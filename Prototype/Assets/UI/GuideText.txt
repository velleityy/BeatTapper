BeatTapper — Game Mechanics Guide

Goal
Hit notes in time with the music to build combo, increase score, and maintain accuracy.

Controls
D F J K for four lanes.
Tap when the note crosses the hit line.
Hold notes: press at start, keep held, release at end (if implemented).

Judgement Windows
Perfect > Great > Good > Miss.

HUD
Score (top right), Combo (top right), Judge text near the hit line.
